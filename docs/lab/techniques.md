# Nine worked LP models

The nine worked models of chapter 4: production, transportation, diets, multi-period planning, blending and ratios. Change the data with the sliders: the simplex method, in the browser, recomputes the optimal solution, the optimal value and the shadow prices. The buttons at the top right choose the model.
