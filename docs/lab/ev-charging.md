# Minimum-cost overnight charging

Three vans to charge in the six hours from midnight to 6 am: charging follows the cheap hours as far as the meter limit allows. Change the limit, the energy needed and the prices and see the profile, the cost and the shadow prices. ▶ next to a slider makes it move by itself.
